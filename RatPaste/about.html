Who / What is Rat Paste


Rat Paste is a rock/metal band from Washington State consisting of members Brody (guitar, bass, etc..) and Rowan (drums). Mixing genres such as punk, rock, nu metal, folk, synth, and pop. Brody calls it "Rat Metal".

We released our first songs BEER & Chronically Online on October 15th 2025 after the original lineup fell apart around July of the same year.

On April 18th 2026, we released their first unironic song Luna featuring a guest vocalist, James.

Rumor has it that "the world CAN wait for their next album to drop!"
